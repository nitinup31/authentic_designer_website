Who We Are

Established in 1996, Authentic Designers has earned a prominent name in the market by offering a comprehensive array of Packaging Machines in wide varieties. Apart from this, we are offering Automatic Form Fill and Seal Machines, Automatic Packaging Machines, and Filling Machines. These products are also exported to international countries. We are committed to delivering high-speed functionality, with fully automated features and compact size. Our main aim is to fully satisfy our customers, ensuring the best results in every task we complete. Owning to an advanced manufacturing unit that is situated in the best locality of Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India, we are able to process excellent range of product that completely adhere to latest market & industrial norms.

Our Services

We provide top-quality machine manufacturing services.

Automatic Pouch Packing Machine

The Automatic Pouch Packing Machine is a state-of-the-art solution for efficient packaging in industries like food, beverages, and pharmaceuticals. Designed for speed and precision, it handles various pouch types with ease. Its robust build and advanced automation ensure reliability, enhancing productivity while maintaining product safety and quality standards.

Automatic Labeling Machine

The Automatic Labeling Machine streamlines the labeling process with precision and speed. Ideal for industries handling bottles, jars, and containers, it ensures consistent labeling accuracy. With cutting-edge sensors and adjustable settings, it enhances production efficiency. This versatile machine is perfect for pharmaceuticals, beverages, and consumer goods industries, ensuring high-quality output.

Powder Filling Machine

A powder filling machine is an automated device used in industries to efficiently and accurately fill powders into containers. These machines are equipped with digital controls, offering precision, speed, and consistency. Ideal for pharmaceutical, food, and chemical sectors, they ensure high-quality packaging with minimal human intervention and waste."

Premade Pouch Machine

Premade pouch machines streamline packaging processes with precision and efficiency. Designed for versatility, they handle various pouch sizes and materials, ensuring consistent sealing and filling. Ideal for industries like food, pharmaceuticals, and cosmetics, these machines enhance productivity while maintaining quality standards in a compact, automated setup.
